# NexHum – HR Analytics Case Study

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of **NexHum**, an HR Analytics case competition organized by Symbiosis Institute of Business Management, Nagpur.

The project focuses on applying data analytics to HR-related business challenges, identifying patterns in workforce data, evaluating potential biases, and translating analytical findings into meaningful business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze workforce and recruitment-related data.
- Identify patterns and disparities across employee groups.
- Examine the influence of factors such as gender, college tier, and other relevant variables.
- Evaluate potential biases in the selection process.
- Develop data-driven insights and recommendations to support fairer HR decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data cleaning, data manipulation, exploratory data analysis, and statistical analysis.
- **Pandas:** Data processing and analysis.
- **Data Visualization:** Communicating analytical findings.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaned and prepared the dataset for analysis.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Examined workforce patterns and relevant variables.
3. **Statistical Analysis:** Investigated relationships and potential disparities across different groups.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Created visualizations to communicate key findings.
5. **Business Insights:** Interpreted the results and developed recommendations.

## Key Focus Areas

- Recruitment and selection patterns.
- Gender representation and selection rates.
- Impact of educational background and college tiers.
- Potential sources of bias in hiring decisions.
- Data-driven HR recommendations.

## Project Deliverables

- Python analysis and supporting code.
- Interactive HR analytics dashboard.
- Key findings and business recommendations.
- Competition presentation.

## Learning Outcomes

- Applied analytical techniques to a real-world HR business problem.
- Improved understanding of recruitment metrics and workforce analytics.
- Developed experience in translating statistical findings into business insights.
- Strengthened data storytelling and dashboard development skills.
